Forecast-led guidance for petroleum levy decisions

Fiscal Lens forecasts commodity prices and turns them into a recommended petroleum levy, balancing what households can afford against revenue targets and IMF commitments.

Forecasts oil, gold and other commodity prices from historical data, with the accuracy of every forecast reported.
